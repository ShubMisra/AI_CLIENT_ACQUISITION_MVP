## What we learned from this first public sample

1. Low advertised budgets regularly accompany broad requirements. A sourcing agent that optimizes for number of AI projects found will feed us a large amount of unattractive work.
2. The best short-term offer may be **a narrowly defined paid diagnostic, repair or pilot** rather than a fully integrated AI application.
3. Not every attractive-budget project is founder-capacity-compatible. Big inventory/CV programs are partnership opportunities, not immediate solo commitments.
4. Explicitly published demand should be evaluated separately from company directories. Neither category should be treated as a verified client until qualification.

**Operating experiment:** Review these 10 source pages, select up to three genuinely open and technically feasible opportunities, and tailor each response to a specific scope/metric. Record every rejection and buyer response in the audit log. Do not send generic mass proposals.
